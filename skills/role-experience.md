# Role Experience Direction

## Student Experience
Should feel:
- welcoming
- calm
- simple
- emotionally safe

Students focus on:
- room life
- tickets
- complaints
- communication

---

# Manager Experience
Should feel:
- operational
- efficient
- organized
- controlled

Managers focus on:
- coordination
- approvals
- maintenance
- occupancy

---

# Admin Experience
Should feel:
- system-wide
- strategic
- powerful
- structured

Admins focus on:
- governance
- analytics
- configuration
- monitoring

---

# AI Experience
AI should feel:
- invisible
- integrated
- intelligent
- operationally useful

Avoid:
- gimmicky AI branding
- fake assistant personalities
