# Image Direction Skill

## Image Philosophy
Imagery should feel cinematic, architectural, and emotionally immersive.

Images must support the atmosphere of modern student living.

## Preferred Imagery
- modern dormitory interiors
- luxury student residences
- architectural hallways
- lounge spaces
- warm lighting
- study environments
- modern communal areas
- minimal architecture
- premium hospitality aesthetics

## Image Style
- soft natural lighting
- warm neutral tones
- realistic photography
- cinematic framing
- elegant composition
- editorial quality

## Composition
- layered image overlap
- partial image cropping
- floating visual panels
- offset grids
- immersive full-width sections

## Avoid
- stock-photo smiling teams
- generic startup illustrations
- cartoon graphics
- neon visuals
- fake AI renders
- low-quality dashboard mockups

## Image Sources
Prefer:
- Unsplash
- Pexels
- real architectural photography
- luxury hospitality visuals

Use large immersive images throughout the landing page.
## Motion-Aware Imagery

Images should support cinematic scroll choreography.

Prefer:
- architectural depth
- leading lines
- atmospheric lighting
- foreground/background separation
- immersive spatial framing

Images should work naturally with:
- parallax movement
- zoom transitions
- layered masking reveals
- sticky storytelling sections
- cinematic scroll transitions
