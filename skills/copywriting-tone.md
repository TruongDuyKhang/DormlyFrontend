# Dormly Copywriting Tone

## Tone
Copy should feel:
- calm
- premium
- architectural
- emotionally restrained
- cinematic

Avoid:
- startup hype
- aggressive marketing
- sales-heavy language

---

# Preferred Language

Use:
- spatial language
- operational calmness
- hospitality-inspired wording

Examples:
- "quiet operational rhythm"
- "student living experience"
- "residence coordination"
- "calm operational flow"

Avoid:
- "revolutionary"
- "game-changing"
- "best platform ever"
- "supercharge"

---

# Sentence Style

Prefer:
- short cinematic statements
- elegant rhythm
- restrained confidence

Avoid:
- long technical dumps
- feature spam
- startup buzzwords
