==================================================

PROJECT:
DORMLY — Modern Dormitory Management Platform

==================================================

IMPORTANT PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Dormly is split into 2 major application experiences:

1. app/(student)
→ student-facing experience

2. app/(platform)
→ manager/admin operational platform

The landing page exists separately in:

3. app/(landing)

==================================================

CORE PRODUCT DIRECTION

Dormly combines:
- residence operations
- room management
- maintenance workflows
- complaints
- notifications
- communication
- AI-assisted automation
- operational analytics

into one connected operational ecosystem.

==================================================

EXPERIENCE GOAL

The product should feel closer to:
- a luxury residence platform
- modern hospitality systems
- architectural digital products
- premium operational software

NOT:
- generic SaaS startup
- crypto dashboard
- enterprise ERP
- template admin UI

==================================================

LANDING PAGE DIRECTION

The landing page should feel:
- cinematic
- immersive
- architectural
- editorial
- emotionally restrained
- visually premium

Scrolling should feel like:
- moving through architecture
- walking through premium residence spaces
- one connected visual journey

Use:
- cinematic storytelling
- layered compositions
- overlapping imagery
- atmospheric transitions
- scroll choreography
- pinned storytelling
- premium motion systems
- immersive imagery

Avoid:
- isolated sections
- startup layouts
- repetitive feature grids
- generic SaaS aesthetics
- obvious AI-generated structure

==================================================

DASHBOARD DIRECTION

Platform dashboards should feel:
- operational
- breathable
- structured
- premium
- calm
- architectural

Use:
- asymmetrical layouts
- restrained metrics
- layered visual hierarchy
- premium glass surfaces
- editorial spacing
- cinematic transitions

Avoid:
- crowded ERP interfaces
- crypto aesthetics
- equal card grids
- analytics overload

==================================================

STUDENT EXPERIENCE DIRECTION

Student interfaces should feel:
- welcoming
- calm
- simple
- emotionally safe
- modern
- intuitive

Avoid:
- operational complexity
- dense management UI
- enterprise styling

==================================================

MOTION DIRECTION

Motion should feel:
- cinematic
- smooth
- physical
- spatial
- immersive

Use:
- GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scrolling
- Framer Motion
- staggered reveals
- image masking
- parallax systems
- connected section transitions
- environmental depth
- layered choreography

Avoid:
- flashy gimmicks
- chaotic movement
- excessive bouncing
- disconnected transitions

==================================================

VISUAL LANGUAGE

The entire product should emotionally communicate:

"A premium modern student living experience."

rather than:

"A generic dormitory management dashboard."
